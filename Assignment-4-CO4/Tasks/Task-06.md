# Task 6 – Reinforcement-Learning Energy Controller

Model observations, Off/Low/High actions, energy and comfort reward, exploration and discount. Compare rule-based, imitation, Q-learning, SARSA and model-based alternatives. Give complete pseudocode and four manual updates. Simulate 15-minute control, compare at least 20 seeds with fixed-rule baseline and attack the reward with an adversarial price/occupancy pattern.
